Cryptography & Number Theory

A direct spinoff of Quantum Computing's still-locked Shor's algorithm lesson: what RSA actually is, why factoring is believed to be hard, and exactly what a quantum computer threatens when it threatens "encryption." Starts from modular arithmetic and Euler's theorem, builds RSA and Diffie-Hellman from real worked examples with small numbers, then follows the thread out to elliptic curves and post-quantum cryptography.
