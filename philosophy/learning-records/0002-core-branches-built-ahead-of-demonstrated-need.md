# Core branches 1–9 built on request, ahead of demonstrated need

The user asked for "philosophy courses 1 through 9" — the nine placeholder cards that had sat locked in the
index since the topic was scaffolded. All nine now exist. This is a coverage event, not yet a learning event:
nothing here records demonstrated understanding, because the lessons have been written but not worked through.

**Why it matters for next session.** [[0001-arrived-via-logic-extensions.md]] established the real zone of
proximal development as formal-logic-adjacent, and that still holds — so these nine were written to route
*through* the formal material rather than around it, rather than being taught as a generic intro survey:

- Lesson 1 frames the whole field as question-triage (empirical / conceptual / normative), with the normative
  branch landing directly on the is-ought gap the user already met in lesson 10.
- Lesson 2 ties the JTB truth condition to `Kp → p` from lesson 11, and treats Gettier as the counterexample
  method rather than as trivia.
- Lesson 3 is framed as the answer to "where do deontic logic's normative premises come from?"
- Lesson 5 was **deliberately not** a repeat of the Logic curriculum. Since the user has all 16 Logic lessons,
  it teaches the five philosophical *moves* (counterexample, thought experiment, charity, reductio, reflective
  equilibrium) and links back to Logic for the formal machinery instead of re-teaching validity and soundness.
- Lesson 9 doubles as spaced review: each thinker links to wherever their move appears in lessons 1–8.

**What to watch for next time.** The open question is whether the user actually wants this breadth or arrived
here by completionism about the locked cards. Signal to read: which lesson they ask a follow-up question about.
A question from lesson 5 or 7 would confirm the logic-adjacent reading; a question from 6 or 8 would mean the
zone has genuinely widened and [[../MISSION.md]] may need revisiting.
