Topic overview

Quantum Computing

Builds directly on Quantum Mechanics's formalism — state vectors, the Born rule, entanglement, unitary evolution — and asks what happens when you treat those as a computing model instead of a physical theory. Gates, circuits, interference as the actual source of speedup (not "trying every answer at once"), and the landmark algorithms: Deutsch-Jozsa, Grover's, Shor's.
