PD Control Cheat Sheet

The compressed version of lesson 4 — the equation, the units, and a symptom → cause → fix table for tuning a Go2 stand-up mission.

The control law

tau = Kp * (q_des - q) + Kd * (dq_des - dq) + tau_ff
SymbolNameUnitsWhat it does
qpositionradcurrent joint angle
q_desdesired positionradtarget joint angle
dqvelocityrad/scurrent joint angular velocity
dq_desdesired velocityrad/starget angular velocity (usually 0 at a held pose)
Kpposition gainN·m/radstiffness — push per unit of position error
Kdvelocity gainN·m/(rad/s)damping — resistance per unit of velocity
tau_fffeedforward torqueN·ma known load (e.g. gravity) supplied directly
tauoutput torqueN·mwhat the motor actually applies

This is the exact field layout of the Go2's motor_cmd struct (q, dq, kp, kd, tau), and the convention traces back to Ben Katz's MIT thesis on quasi-direct-drive actuators for legged robots.

Symptom → likely cause → what to try

Symptom in the log/plotLikely causeTry
Settles short of target and stays there (steady droop)Kp too low relative to a constant load; no tau_ffRaise Kp (shrinks it, never zeroes it), or add tau_ff (can zero it)
Rings / oscillates before settling, or never settlesKd too low relative to KpRaise Kd
Reaches target very slowly, feels "sluggish"Kd too high relative to KpLower Kd, or raise Kp to compensate
Overshoots once, then settlesKp high relative to Kd (underdamped)Raise Kd a little, or lower Kp

Real numbers from the reference project

Gains (original)KP=60.0, KD=5.0 (flat across all 12 joints) — copied from Unitree's own go2_stand_example.cpp
Feedforward (original)tau = 0 for every joint — none used
Mission tolerance0.05 rad per joint
Captured failure (original)rear-right hip joint (index 6): max_joint_error = 0.181 rad during ramp, 0.169 rad during hold
Gains (current)same KP=60, KD=5, plus KI=6.0 integral gain feeding the tau feedforward field (clamped ±15 N·m); integral resets to 0 when the ramp ends
Result (current)mission completed — max joint error ≈0.028 rad, verified reproducible over 3 fresh-sim runs

Source (private repo): stand-mission-report.json / stand-mission-report-tuned.json — see also the tuning history in run_stand_mission.py's own docstring.

The original numbers are still worth keeping: they're the honest baseline this whole lesson is built on — the point where raising Kp/Kd alone runs into a hard limit, and where adding something beyond pure PD (feedforward or integral) becomes necessary.
