Key rules of thumb

  • No single sensor gives ground truth. What your code reads is already an estimate.
  • Gyroscope: trustworthy short-term, drifts long-term (bias accumulates under integration).
  • Accelerometer: no drift, but noisy and gravity-confounded moment-to-moment.
  • Fusing both (complementary/Kalman filter) covers each one's weak spot.
  • Any smoothing filter trades noise rejection against reaction lag — there's no setting with both.
